Frequently Asked
What transcript formats are supported?
Any plain text works — Zoom auto-transcripts, Otter.ai exports, Google Meet captions, hand-written notes, or even a quick bullet list of what was covered. The AI adapts to whatever you paste in.
How does it assign action item owners?
The AI reads the transcript for who said they would do what, and matches those references to the attendee names you provide. Owners default to TBD if a name can't be matched.
Is my meeting data kept private?
Transcript content is sent to the AI for processing and is not stored on our servers. We recommend removing any highly sensitive personal data before pasting if that is a concern.
What does the engagement score measure?
The score (1-10) reflects how productive and focused your meeting was based on the number of concrete decisions made, action items created, questions raised, and discussion depth. Use it to track meeting quality over time.
