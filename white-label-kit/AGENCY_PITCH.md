## Objection Handlers

**"We already use ChatGPT / other AI tools."**
> "Totally — this isn't about replacing what your team uses internally. It's about giving your clients a branded AI portal they pay you for monthly. ChatGPT doesn't put your logo on it or let you bill for it."

**"We don't have devs to maintain this."**
> "The deployment is one command and the rebranding is one script — no coding needed. And because it's deployed on Vercel, maintenance is essentially zero. I'll walk you through it on a 30-minute call."

**"$5,000 is too much."**
> "Totally fair — that's why we have a $1,000 Starter tier with 3 tools. If you add just one client at $200/month, you break even in 5 months. Want to start there and upgrade later?"

**"We'd want to build this ourselves."**
> "You absolutely could — it'd take a developer 3–6 months and $15,000+. This gives you a working, deployed product today and lets your team focus on client work instead of infra."

**"How do I know this is legit / will work?"**
> "Happy to do a live screen-share demo of the full suite, show you the source code structure, and walk through the rebrand script together before you commit. Zero pressure."

**"What happens if you shut down or disappear?"**
> "You own the source code outright after purchase. The license is perpetual. Even if Dizzy Inc stopped operating tomorrow, your deployment keeps running on your Vercel account with your API key. You're never dependent on us."
